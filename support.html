Support

Connect Riders mobile app

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Need help with Connect Riders? Check the answers below first. If your question is not covered, email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Email: connect.ridders.support@gmail.com

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include your device model, operating system version, the app version (shown at the bottom of Profile → About), a description of what happened, and screenshots if you have them.

Account

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password and enter the email address you registered with. We will send you a link to set a new password. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

How do I change the app language?

Open the Profile tab and choose Change Language. English, Portuguese, and Spanish are available.

How do I delete my account?

You can request deletion of your Connect Riders account and all related data at any time:

  1. Open the app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner, then Delete Account.
  3. Confirm by tapping Request deletion. You will be signed out.

If you can no longer sign in, email connect.ridders.support@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Account deletion request”.

Deletion removes your profile, posts, comments, routes, events, and subscription records from our systems. It is processed within 30 days and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings as described below. See our Privacy Policy for details on data retention.

Connect Riders Pro subscription

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Connect Riders Pro.
  • Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Connect Riders Pro.
  • You can also tap Manage on the Profile tab inside the app.

After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro is not active

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to purchase, then open the Profile tab and tap Restore Purchases. If Pro still does not activate, email us with your order receipt from Apple or Google.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds follow their policies and we cannot issue them directly. Request one from reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or Google Play support (Android).

Routes and location

Route recording stops or skips points

  • Allow location access for Connect Riders. On iPhone, choose Always so recording continues while your phone is locked. On Android, allow location all the time.
  • Turn off battery saver or battery optimisation for the app during a ride.
  • Keep GPS enabled. The app warns you if location is switched off mid-recording.

Nearby content does not show anything

Nearby uses your current location and shows posts, routes, and events within about 100 km. Check that location permission is granted and that your profile location is up to date.

Reporting a problem or a user

To report inappropriate content, a user, or a bug, email us with a link or the username involved and a short description. We review every report.
